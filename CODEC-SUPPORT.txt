编码与传输兼容性 / Codec and transport compatibility
===================================================
项目版本 / Project version: v1.35
组件 / Components: MediaMTX v1.21.0-r11, HLS.js v1.7.3, Go v1.27.1

中文
----

编码范围
服务器不转码；浏览器必须具备输入编码、profile 和 bit depth 对应的接收与解码能力。
项目播放器识别 H.264/AVC、H.265/HEVC、AV1、VP9 视频，以及 AAC、Opus 音频。

  Manifest codec             识别结果
  avc1 / avc3                 H.264
  hvc1 / hev1                 HEVC
  av01                       AV1
  vp09                       VP9
  mp4a                       AAC
  opus                       Opus

WHEP 与 HLS/MSE 的支持必须分别检测，不能用一条路径通过推断另一条可用。
HEVC、AV1、VP9 尤其依赖浏览器和设备能力。H.264 的 WebRTC 输入必须关闭 B 帧。
WebRTC 编码兼容性依据：
  [MediaMTX WebRTC 特性](https://mediamtx.org/docs/features/webrtc-specific-features)

WHIP 常用 Opus；WHEP 播放器显式协商 stereo=1 和 sprop-stereo=1，并把分开到达的
视频/音频轨合并到同一 MediaStream。RTMP 常用 AAC，而 WHEP 不支持 AAC；此时
WHEP 可能只有画面，使用兼容的 HLS/MSE 播放路径才能保留 AAC 声音。网页初始静音，
需点击“开启声音”；服务器不会将 AAC 转为 Opus。WebRTC 音频范围依据：
  [MediaMTX WebRTC 播放](https://mediamtx.org/docs/read/webrtc)

推流与观看路径
  WHIP： http://<LAN_IP>:8889/live/whip
         Bearer Token 为 obs:<随机推流码>
  RTMP： rtmp://<LAN_IP>:1935
         Stream key 为 live?user=obs&pass=<随机推流码>
  HLS：  https://<PUBLIC_DOMAIN>[:PUBLIC_HTTPS_PORT]/live/index.m3u8
  WHEP： https://<PUBLIC_DOMAIN>[:PUBLIC_HTTPS_PORT]/rtc/live/whep

8889/1935 仅绑定启动识别的 WHIP_IP，发布同时校验密码及所选网卡真实局域网段
加本机 /32；该检查以服务器看到的源 IP 为准，不能识别被 NAT/代理改写的原始
外部来源。公网网页不代理 WHIP 发布。WHEP 信令走 HTTPS，媒体走 UDP/TCP 8189
的 ICE/DTLS-SRTP 通道，不扩大发布权限。详细端口、IPv4 ICE 白名单和 NAT 要求
见 DNS-SETUP.txt、FIREWALL.txt；OBS 参数见 OBS-COMPATIBILITY.txt。

  AUTO                    有 RTCPeerConnection 时优先尝试 WHEP，否则使用 HLS
  AV1/VP9 + AAC           兼容 MSE 时可手动 LL-HLS 保留声音
  AV1/VP9 + Opus          浏览器声明相应 WebRTC 能力时可用 WHEP
  AV1/VP9 不支持 MSE      仍可独立尝试支持相应 WebRTC 编码的 WHEP
  HLS 挂起或失败          WHEP 独立建立；HLS metadata 不是前置依赖
  无直播                  WHEP 有界重试；HLS 按 5/10/20/30/60 秒退避

每个新 WHEP 会话并行获取 HLS 主清单作为编码提示。手动、AUTO 和内部重建均不
等待该请求；建立期限为 15 秒。手动超时或编码不支持时保留 WHEP 选择并允许重试，
AUTO 保留受控回退。旧异步请求失效；晚到的旧会话响应只清理自己的会话。

MediaMTX 兼容行为
- 首个 WebRTC 轨道收集时限为 15 秒，允许软件编码器初始化。
- AV1 OBU 会归一化；只在真实 KEY_FRAME 缺少序列头时补回缓存的 sequence header。
- gortmplib v1.0.2 的随附修补会忽略 Enhanced RTMP 空 AV1 sequence-start，
  等待后续有效 av1C，避免把占位配置当 EOF。
- AOM AV1 关键帧间隔应显式设为 1～2 秒，不能用 0/自动，以便 HLS 定期切段。
- VP9 原样路由；WHEP 用 VP9/90000 协商，LL-HLS 用实际 vp09 codec string 检查
  MSE/MediaCapabilities。
- RTMP 可接收不适合 WebRTC 的重排/时间戳结构，但不会让该结构自动兼容 WHEP，
  也不能解决 OBS 编码队列超时或浏览器持续解码问题。

LL-HLS 与弱网模式
服务端保留 24 个媒体段，目标媒体段 2 秒、CMAF part 1 秒；实际切段受关键帧和
时间戳影响。1 秒 part 适配 24/25/30/50/60 等整数帧率的整帧周期。
播放器参数独立于 OBS 编码参数：

  模式              lowLatencyMode   目标延迟   buffer 上限*  最大播放速率
  Normal            true             6 秒       10/15 秒      1.05x
  bandwidth-loss    true             12 秒      16/24 秒      1.00x
  RTT Weak          false（整段）     12 秒      16/24 秒      1.00x
  * maxBufferLength / maxMaxBufferLength；不是实测缓冲量。

Weak 的实际连续前向缓冲首次达到 8 秒才进入 READY；之后达到 6 秒可维持，低于
6 秒时在监测采样中退出 READY。目标延迟、配置和 seek 成功都不能替代实际缓冲量。

bandwidth-loss 基本条件为 ratio<1.2 且 buffer<4 秒，连续 6 个风险样本确认，
健康样本清零；3 个有效下降趋势或 3 个严重缺缓冲网络样本也可提前触发保护。
ratio 是估计可用带宽与流码率之比。RTT 分类需要健康请求开销 baseline、连续
4 个相对恶化样本、带宽余量、没有近期网络错误，且缓冲受压。

Fast recovery：ratio>=1.7、buffer>=6 秒，20 个健康样本及 15 秒无卡顿/网络错误。
Stable recovery：ratio>=1.5、buffer>=7 秒，30 个健康样本及 30 秒无卡顿/网络错误。
安全缓冲谷值最多保留既有证据 3000 ms，不增加健康样本；恢复还要求 Weak 已实际
READY。延迟仍高但吞吐和缓冲健康时也可能恢复 Normal，不等于外部网络已无延迟。

HLS.js 原始资源通过 bufferController 扩展获得 SourceBuffer append 超时归属保护：
完成、错误、替换或销毁会退休旧回调；仅当前缓冲区/操作仍在 updating 时允许超时
恢复。15 秒 append watchdog 保留，避免过期回调对空闲缓冲区执行 abort。

直播已播放至少 8 秒、音频/视频清单发生非致命超时且实际前向缓冲低于 6 秒时，
立即进入既有 bandwidth-loss 保护，停止加速并尝试一次有界安全回退。保护可能产生
短暂 seek、内容回退或卡顿；持续带宽低于流码率或长时间断网仍可能停播。该策略
不转码、不降低 OBS 码率，也不修复路由器/NAT 的 TCP 重传。

验证边界
必须观察实际 currentTime、连续缓冲、解码帧和音视频表现。FRAG_BUFFERED 或
WHEP connected/RTP 持续增长单独出现都不代表持续解码成功；手动 LL-HLS 和手动
WHEP 应分别验证。播放器会提示 HLS/MSE/HTTP/WebRTC 错误、静音、缺音轨和
AAC/WHEP 不兼容，遇到无画面/声音请结合这些状态诊断。

English
-------

Codec scope
The server does not transcode. The browser must support reception and decoding
of the input codec, profile and bit depth. The project player recognizes H.264/
AVC, H.265/HEVC, AV1 and VP9 video, plus AAC and Opus audio.

  Manifest codec             Recognition
  avc1 / avc3                 H.264
  hvc1 / hev1                 HEVC
  av01                       AV1
  vp09                       VP9
  mp4a                       AAC
  opus                       Opus

Test WHEP and HLS/MSE separately; success with one does not prove the other works.
HEVC, AV1 and VP9 depend especially on browser/device capabilities. Disable
H.264 B-frames for WebRTC. Codec compatibility reference:
  [MediaMTX WebRTC features](https://mediamtx.org/docs/features/webrtc-specific-features)

WHIP normally uses Opus. The WHEP player explicitly negotiates stereo=1 and
sprop-stereo=1 and combines separately arriving video/audio tracks into one
MediaStream. RTMP commonly uses AAC, which WHEP does not support. WHEP may then
have video only; a compatible HLS/MSE path preserves AAC audio. The page starts
muted; click the sound button. The server never converts AAC to Opus.
WebRTC audio reference:
  [MediaMTX WebRTC playback](https://mediamtx.org/docs/read/webrtc)

Publishing and viewing paths
  WHIP: http://<LAN_IP>:8889/live/whip
        Bearer Token: obs:<random-stream-key>
  RTMP: rtmp://<LAN_IP>:1935
        Stream key: live?user=obs&pass=<random-stream-key>
  HLS:  https://<PUBLIC_DOMAIN>[:PUBLIC_HTTPS_PORT]/live/index.m3u8
  WHEP: https://<PUBLIC_DOMAIN>[:PUBLIC_HTTPS_PORT]/rtc/live/whep

Ports 8889/1935 bind only to the detected WHIP_IP. Publishing checks both the
password and the actual selected interface subnet plus the local /32 address,
as seen by the server; NAT/proxy source rewriting can hide the original external
source. The public site does not proxy WHIP publishing. WHEP signalling uses HTTPS;
media uses ICE/DTLS-SRTP on UDP/TCP 8189 without expanding publishing permissions.
See DNS-SETUP.txt and FIREWALL.txt for ports, the IPv4 ICE allowlist and NAT;
see OBS-COMPATIBILITY.txt for encoder settings.

  AUTO                    WHEP first if RTCPeerConnection exists; HLS otherwise
  AV1/VP9 + AAC           Manual LL-HLS can preserve audio if MSE supports it
  AV1/VP9 + Opus          WHEP if the browser advertises that WebRTC codec
  AV1/VP9 without MSE     WHEP remains an independent option if its codec is supported
  HLS hangs/fails          WHEP setup does not depend on HLS metadata
  No live stream          Bounded WHEP retries; HLS backoff at 5/10/20/30/60 seconds

Every new WHEP session fetches HLS master metadata in parallel as an advisory
codec hint. Manual, AUTO and internal rebuilds do not wait for it. The connection
deadline is 15 seconds. Manual timeout or codec failure retains the WHEP choice
and allows retry; AUTO retains controlled fallback. Old asynchronous requests
become invalid, and late old-session responses clean up only their own session.

MediaMTX compatibility behavior
- The WebRTC track-gather deadline is 15 seconds to allow encoder initialization.
- AV1 OBUs are normalized. A cached sequence header is restored only when a real
  KEY_FRAME lacks one.
- The bundled gortmplib v1.0.2 patch ignores an empty Enhanced RTMP AV1
  sequence-start and waits for valid av1C instead of treating the placeholder as EOF.
- Set AOM AV1 keyframe intervals explicitly to 1–2 seconds, not 0/automatic, so HLS
  can segment regularly.
- VP9 is routed without conversion. WHEP negotiates VP9/90000; LL-HLS tests the
  actual vp09 codec string through MSE/MediaCapabilities.
- RTMP can accept reordering/timestamp structures unsuitable for WebRTC. It does
  not make those structures compatible with WHEP or fix OBS encoder queue
  timeouts or browser decoding continuity.

LL-HLS and weak-network mode
The server retains 24 media segments with a 2-second segment target and 1-second
CMAF part target. Actual segmentation depends on keyframes and timestamps.
One-second parts align with integer frame periods at 24/25/30/50/60 FPS.
Player profiles are independent of OBS encoding settings:

  Profile           lowLatencyMode       Target delay  Buffer limits*  Max rate
  Normal            true                 6 s           10/15 s         1.05x
  bandwidth-loss    true                 12 s          16/24 s         1.00x
  RTT Weak          false (full segments) 12 s          16/24 s         1.00x
  * maxBufferLength / maxMaxBufferLength, not measured buffered media.

Weak first becomes READY with at least 8 seconds of actual continuous forward
buffer. It remains ready at 6 seconds or more; a monitoring sample below 6 seconds
clears readiness. Target latency, configuration and successful seeking do not
substitute for actual buffered media.

The basic bandwidth-loss path requires ratio<1.2 and buffer<4 s for 6 consecutive
risk samples; healthy samples reset the count. Three valid draining samples or
three severe-starvation network samples can trigger earlier protection. Ratio
means estimated available bandwidth divided by stream bitrate. RTT classification
needs a healthy request-overhead baseline, 4 consecutive elevated relative
samples, bandwidth headroom, no recent network errors and buffer pressure.

Fast recovery requires ratio>=1.7, buffer>=6 s, 20 healthy samples and 15 s without
stalls/network errors. Stable recovery requires ratio>=1.5, buffer>=7 s, 30 healthy
samples and 30 s without stalls/network errors. Safe buffer valleys can preserve
existing evidence for up to 3000 ms without adding healthy samples. Recovery also
requires actual Weak readiness. Healthy throughput and buffer can restore Normal
while external delay remains elevated; recovery does not prove zero network delay.

The original HLS.js asset uses a bufferController extension to bind each
SourceBuffer append timeout to its operation. Completion, error, replacement and
destruction retire old callbacks. Timeout recovery is permitted only for the
current buffer/operation while it is updating. The 15-second append watchdog
remains enabled, avoiding stale aborts against idle buffers.

After at least 8 seconds of live playback, a nonfatal audio/video playlist timeout
with actual forward buffer below 6 seconds immediately activates bandwidth-loss
protection, stops acceleration and attempts one bounded safe backtrack. Protection
can cause a short seek, replayed content or a stall. Sustained bandwidth below the
stream bitrate or a long outage can still stop playback. This policy does not
transcode, lower the OBS bitrate or repair router/NAT TCP retransmissions.

Verification boundary
Observe currentTime, continuous buffered ranges, decoded frames and actual audio/
video. FRAG_BUFFERED alone, or a connected WHEP session with increasing RTP
counters, does not prove continuous decoding. Verify manual LL-HLS and manual
WHEP independently. The player reports HLS/MSE/HTTP/WebRTC errors, muting, missing
audio tracks and AAC/WHEP incompatibility; use these states when diagnosing
missing video or sound.
